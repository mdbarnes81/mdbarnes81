App models

    Left and right arrow keys move between app models, keys 1 to 6 jump to one, Home resets the camera and Escape returns to the overview. Everything shown here is also available in the App models list, the details panel and the Table view.

    Help and shortcuts

    The 3D scene is one way in. Every fact is also in the App models list, the details panel and the Table view, so nothing depends on the canvas, on colour or on motion.

    Getting around

    Tab
    Move through the views, tools, app models and details.
    ← →
    In the view tabs, switch view. With the 3D view focused, step to the previous or next app model.
    1 to 6
    With the 3D view focused, jump straight to an app model.
    Home
    With the 3D view focused, reset the camera.
    Esc
    Return to the overview of all six.
    Pointer
    Select a structure to travel to it: a pulse runs back to the centre and out along the new Copilot path while the camera sweeps round. Drag to orbit and scroll or pinch to zoom.

    Comfort

    Motion turns camera flights, particles and rotation on or off. It starts off when your device asks for reduced motion, and views then dissolve instead of flying. Your choice is remembered. Light theme switches the whole page, including the scene. The guided tour can be stopped at any time and stops by itself when you take over.

    Reading the scene

    The centre is VS Code with GitHub Copilot. The ring around it is the HVE Core workflow: Research, Plan, Implement and Review. Each of the six structures is an app model, labelled with the icon of its Microsoft product, whose four tiers are identity and governance at the base, then hosting, data and front end. The arc from the centre to each structure is its VS Code and GitHub Copilot path.
